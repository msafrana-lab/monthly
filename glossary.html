Glossary

Key concepts explained

The concepts used in the monthly letter and its pages, updated every month.

Defensive stocks
Shares in companies whose sales depend little on the economic cycle (healthcare, food) and which pay regular dividends.
Diversifying strategies
Strategies entrusted to managers whose performance depends little on the direction of equity and bond markets.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rates: a duration of 10 years implies a price fall of around 10% if rates rise by one percentage point.
El Niño
A periodic warming of surface waters in the tropical Pacific, which alters weather patterns across much of the globe.
Futures contract
A commitment to buy or sell a commodity or asset at a future date, at a price agreed today.
Interest rate differential
The difference in yield between two currencies or two borrowers. When it widens in favour of the dollar, capital has an incentive to leave the franc.
Policy rate
The rate at which a central bank remunerates or lends to commercial banks. It anchors short-term interest rates across the economy.
Rare earths
A group of metals essential to electronics, electric-motor magnets and wind turbines. China dominates their production and refining.
Safe haven
An asset investors buy when anxiety rises, such as the Swiss franc, gold or certain government bonds.
Spot price
The price paid for immediate delivery, as opposed to the price of a futures contract. For oil, physical Brent delivered to refiners.
Two-year yield
The yield on a two-year government bond. It largely reflects the policy rates expected over that period.
VIX index
A gauge of expected volatility on the US equity market, derived from S&P 500 option prices. Its long-term average is around 19 to 20.
